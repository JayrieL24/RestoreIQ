# RestoreIQ before-and-after image library

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and saved in `public/Before&After`.

Four matched cases, eight final images:

- `carpet-before.png` / `carpet-after.png`
- `drywall-before.png` / `drywall-after.png`
- `hardwood-before.png` / `hardwood-after.png`
- `mould-before.png` / `mould-after.png`

The before images were prompted as restrained contractor documentation photography: normal American residential rooms, eye-level 35mm framing, natural daylight, ordinary materials, subtle believable water damage, and no text, logos, split screens, HDR, CGI surfaces, or disaster spectacle.

Each after image was edited from its corresponding before image with instructions to preserve the exact camera, crop, architecture, fixtures, lighting, and material colors while changing only the completed restoration area and removing job equipment. The four scenarios cover saturated carpet, damaged drywall, cupped hardwood, and localized mould remediation.
